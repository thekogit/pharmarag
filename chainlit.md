# PharmaRAG-Diagnostic

Answers questions from the current FDA labels of five drugs: Ozempic (semaglutide), metformin,
Lipitor (atorvastatin), Eliquis (apixaban) and Zoloft (sertraline). Every claim carries a verbatim
quote from a cited label passage. When the labels don't cover a question, it says so.

Try:
- What is the maximum recommended dosage of Ozempic?
- When should the Eliquis dose be reduced to 2.5 mg twice daily?
- Which liver conditions contraindicate Lipitor?
- What is the Eliquis dose for atrial fibrillation, and what does it cost in Poland?

Research demo. Not medical advice.
